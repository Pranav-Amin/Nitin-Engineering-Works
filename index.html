Who We Are

Built on
Precision.

Nitin Engineering Works is a Mumbai-based precision tool & die manufacturer with decades of experience delivering high-quality stamped components to industries across India.

Located in Goregaon East, our facility houses advanced press machines and inspection equipment, allowing us to maintain tight tolerances from prototype through high-volume production runs.

We specialize in progressive, compound, and fine-blanking dies — producing parts that meet exact drawing specifications, every time.

What We Make

Products &
Services

Progressive Dies
Multi-station tooling for complex stampings. Reduces handling and improves consistency across large production runs.
Compound Dies
Simultaneous blanking and piercing in one stroke. Delivers flat, accurate parts with excellent edge quality.
Precision Blanks
Flat and profiled blanks cut to tight tolerances from sheet coil stock. Supplied ready for secondary operations.
Formed Components
Bending, drawing and forming operations for brackets, channels, clips and structural metal parts.
Tool Repair & Refurbishment
Reconditioning of worn tooling to original specs. Extends die life and reduces replacement costs significantly.
Prototype Tooling
Fast-turnaround prototype dies for design validation before committing to production tooling investment.
